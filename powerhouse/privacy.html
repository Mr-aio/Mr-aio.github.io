Powerhouse privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026. Applies to the Powerhouse Android app and its web version at https://mr-aio.github.io/powerhouse/.

The short version

Powerhouse has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. The only exception is battles with friends: a battle's names and scores are shared with the other player, and synced through a small database so neither of you has to resend turns.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your answers, scores, ATP, streaks, badges, settings and battles are saved in the app's storage on your device (or in your browser's storage for the web version).
  • Your study progress is never sent anywhere.

Battles

  • When you send a battle, the app builds a link that contains the battle name you chose, a random ID for your device, the battle's questions, and both players' results (right or wrong, and answer times).
  • You decide who gets the link by sending it through your own messaging app. Anyone who has the link can open that battle, so pick a battle name you're comfortable sharing.
  • The battle code sits after the "#" in the link. Browsers don't send that part to the web server.
  • After the first link, battle turns sync through a Google Firebase database used only for Powerhouse battles. For each battle it stores the battle code (battle names, questions and results) and a random anonymous ID for each of the two players. There is no email, phone number or sign-in.
  • Only the two players in a battle can read or change it. A battle is deleted from the database when it ends or when either player removes it. A battle with no moves for 30 days is closed and deleted the next time either player opens Powerhouse.

Clipboard and permissions

  • The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste on the Battles screen.
  • It uses two Android permissions: vibration, for haptic feedback you can turn off in Settings, and internet access, for battle sync.

Service providers

Battle sync runs on Google Firebase (anonymous authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. See Firebase privacy and security. Battle links and the web version are served by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. Like most web services, these providers may record technical details such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Settings > Reset progress clears your progress. Each battle has a "Remove this battle" option. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes everything on your device. Removing a battle that is still in progress also deletes it from the database for both players. Finished battles are deleted from the database automatically. To ask for a battle to be deleted sooner, email the developer contact on Powerhouse's Google Play listing.

Children

Powerhouse is made for adults preparing for the MCAT and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, use the developer contact email on Powerhouse's Google Play listing.
