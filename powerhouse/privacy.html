Powerhouse privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026. Applies to the Powerhouse Android app and its web version at https://mr-aio.github.io/powerhouse/.

The short version

Powerhouse has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. The only information that ever leaves it is what you choose to send in a battle link.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your answers, scores, ATP, streaks, badges, settings and battles are saved in the app's storage on your device (or in your browser's storage for the web version).
  • None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no server that receives it.

Battles

  • When you send a battle, the app builds a link that contains the battle name you chose, a random ID for your device, the battle's questions, and both players' results (right or wrong, and answer times).
  • You decide who gets the link by sending it through your own messaging app. Anyone who has the link can open that battle, so pick a battle name you're comfortable sharing.
  • The battle code sits after the "#" in the link. Browsers don't send that part to the web server.

Clipboard and permissions

  • The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste on the Battles screen.
  • The only Android permission it uses is vibration, for haptic feedback you can turn off in Settings.

Web pages and hosting

Battle links and the web version are served by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details such as IP addresses when a page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Settings > Reset progress clears your progress. Each battle has a "Remove this battle" option. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes everything.

Children

Powerhouse is made for adults preparing for the MCAT and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, use the developer contact email on Powerhouse's Google Play listing.
